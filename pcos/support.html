Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary — Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email babycar.zta@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. We reply within a few business days.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud sync. Make an encrypted backup in Settings → Data → Backup and keep the password safe — without it the file cannot be opened, also not by us.

Moving to a new iPhone

Create a backup on the old phone, move the file (AirDrop, Files), then on the new phone open Settings → Data → Restore and enter the password.

Since last visit and the doctor PDF

Open Report, choose "Since last visit" and set the date of your last appointment. Tick the records and categories to include, add "My questions for the doctor", then tap Create PDF. The PDF is made on your device and leaves it only through the share sheet when you choose a recipient.

Labs

Enter values exactly as printed on your lab report, with the unit and the lab's reference range. The app does not convert units and does not say whether a value is normal.

Pro, trial and restore

Pro is available monthly, yearly (7-day free trial for eligible users) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. To restore on a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Sounds, haptics, themes and icon

Settings → Sound & haptics turns sounds, vibration and animations off. Settings → Appearance switches between Light, Plum and Midnight; Settings → App icon changes the Home Screen icon.

Delete everything

Settings → Delete all data erases every record, attachment and photo immediately.
