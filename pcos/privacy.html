Privacy Policy — Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary is built so that your data stays with you. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you enter in Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary is stored locally on your device. We do not run user accounts, we do not have a server that receives your entries, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Camera

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary asks for camera access so you can take photos inside the app. Photos are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded, analyzed on a server, or shared with anyone unless you explicitly use a share/export feature.

Notifications

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary can send local reminders that you set up. These are scheduled on your device; no notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

Optional paid features are sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play — depending on the app, as a one-time purchase or a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details or your Apple/Google account. The app asks the store on your device whether you own the purchase in order to unlock the features you paid for; nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

App lock (Face ID / passcode)

If you turn on the optional app lock, Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary asks iOS to verify you with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The check is performed entirely by iOS; the app only receives a yes/no answer and never has access to your biometric data or passcode. App content is also hidden in the app switcher.

Your health diary stays on your iPhone

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary stores everything you enter — cycle and bleeding entries, daily check-ins, symptoms, lab results and the documents you attach, medications and doses, notes and private photos — only in the app's database on this iPhone. There is no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours; the data is not included in iCloud backups made by the app and is protected by iOS Data Protection. We never receive, sell or share it, and it is never used for advertising.

Doctor summary (PDF) and backup

A PDF summary is created on your device only when you ask for it, and leaves the device only through the system share sheet when you choose a recipient. An optional backup file is encrypted on your device with a password you set (AES-256-GCM); without that password nobody, including us, can open it. Where you store or send these files is your choice.

Not medical advice

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary is a diary of your own records. It does not diagnose, treat or predict anything, is not a medical device and is not a contraceptive. Always discuss your health with a qualified professional.

Delete everything

Settings → Delete all data erases every record, attachment and photo from the app immediately. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Photos

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary asks for access to your photo library only when you choose a picture yourself. The picture you pick is copied into the app's own storage on this device. It is not uploaded, not analysed on a server, and not shared with anyone unless you use a share feature yourself. The app never reads your library in the background and never sees photos you did not pick.

Sound and vibration

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary can play a short tone and vibrate when you record something. Both are produced on the device, both can be turned off in the app's settings, and the phone's silent switch mutes the sound. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

Sound and vibration

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary plays sound effects and uses vibration (haptics) as game feedback. Both are generated on the device and can be switched off in the game. The microphone is never used and nothing is recorded.

Sharing and export

If you use the share or export feature, the content you choose leaves the app only to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). We do not receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Device backups (iCloud, Google) may include app data according to your own backup settings.

Children

Pattern Journal: PCOS Diary is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data in a new way (for example adding crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
