What the app is, and is not

Jamaah Lock shows prayer times, imports a mosque timetable you choose, and, if you ask it to, lets the apps you select rest during prayer windows. Calculated prayer times are an estimate based on the method and place you set; your mosque's own announcement is the reference. The app does not give religious rulings, does not represent any mosque or organisation, and is not a substitute for your own judgement in matters of worship.

Resting apps

Resting apps uses Apple's Screen Time framework at your request. You choose the apps, the mode and the unlock methods, and you can withdraw the permission in iOS Settings at any time. Every window ends on its own at the time shown, and a daily pass is always available in Firm mode. Please do not rely on the app as a safety control for another person's device; it uses the individual authorization only.

Purchases

Pro is an optional one-time purchase or monthly subscription sold by Apple, with Family Sharing. A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple under the standard EULA.

Religious texts

The dhikr phrases in the app are the Arabic formulas in common use, shown exactly. The app contains no Quran text; the khatm plan tracks page numbers only. Fonts and the prayer-time library are used under their open licences, listed in Settings → Licences.
