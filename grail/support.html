GRAIL · HELP DESK

Help with Grail: Sneaker Collection

Grail is a private catalog for the pairs you own. It keeps each pair's condition, age and fit, tells you when an old pair should come out of its box for a look, works out your size in each brand from your own pairs, and prints a dated list for your insurer. Everything is on your iPhone; there is no account.

Something wrong, or a question this page does not answer? Write to babycar.zta@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use; a screenshot helps. A person answers, usually within two working days.

Shelf checks — why my pairs are "overdue"

Old midsoles fail in the box even if the shoes were never worn: polyurethane hydrolyses and crumbles, foams harden and yellow, glue lines let go. Grail looks at the year the pair was made (from the size tag, not when you bought it) and the midsole you picked, and sets how often to look:

MidsoleWatch (every 6 months)High risk (every 3 months)
Polyurethane (PU)6–9 years old10 years and older
Foam (EVA and similar)8–11 years12 years and older
Rubber cupsole15 years and older—
Not sure10 years and older—

Everything else is checked once a year. If a check finds the sole separating or the midsole crumbling, the pair goes to "high risk" whatever its age; yellowing or cracks move a young pair to "watch". A pair you have never checked shows as overdue until you log its first check. These are rules of thumb from collectors, not a lab test — treat them as a reminder to look, not a diagnosis.

Grail shows the due list and a number on the Checks tab. It does not send notifications.

Fit book — how Grail gets "your size"

On each pair, rate how it fits (too tight … too big). A pair that is "roomy" in US 10 tells Grail you would have been right in 9.5. Grail averages that per brand and shows the result in the size system you choose. Brands are grouped by how you typed them, ignoring capitals and spaces. The converter uses one generic chart (US M 9 = US W 10.5 = UK 8 = EU 42.5 = 27 cm); real brands differ by up to a size, which is why your own rated pairs always come first. Tap a brand to keep a note such as "narrow toe box".

Insurance register (Grail Pro)

Vault → Insurance register. Type the policyholder and policy number once; the preview is page one of the real PDF. Each row has the pair, size, condition grade, cost basis (price plus shipping, tax and fees), the replacement value you last entered with its date — or the cost basis if you never entered one, marked "at cost" — and the date of the last shelf check. Totals and a signature line are on the last page. The PDF is made on the phone and handed to the share sheet; Grail keeps no copy. Values are yours, so the register is a schedule you sign, not an appraisal.

Grail Pro

Free, with no limit on the number of pairs: the shelf, condition grades, shelf checks, the fit book and the converter. Grail Pro is an annual auto-renewing subscription that adds values with a dated history, the collection value against cost basis, the insurance register PDF and a CSV export. The price is the one the App Store shows in your country.

Restore on a new iPhone
Vault → Unlock (or Settings → Subscription) → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
Cancel
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Grail, at least 24 hours before renewal. Your pairs and values stay; values are hidden until Pro is active again.
Refunds
Apple handles payments: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Pairs, checks, values and notes are stored on this iPhone only and included in your own iPhone backup. Grail has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads. Settings → Delete all data removes everything at once. Updating from 1.0 keeps every pair; new fields such as year made and midsole start empty.

Money and currency

Choose the currency in Settings. It is a label for amounts you type — Grail never converts or fetches prices, and it is not a marketplace.
