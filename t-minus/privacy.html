Privacy Policy — T-Minus: Days Until Countdown

Last updated: November 23, 2026

T-Minus: Days Until Countdown is built so that your data stays with you. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you enter in T-Minus: Days Until Countdown is stored locally on your device. We do not run user accounts, we do not have a server that receives your entries, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Notifications

T-Minus: Days Until Countdown can send local reminders that you set up. These are scheduled on your device; no notification service of ours is involved.

Contacts (birthdays)

T-Minus: Days Until Countdown can turn birthdays saved in your Contacts into yearly countdowns. This happens only if you open “Birthdays from Contacts” and allow access when iOS asks. The app reads two things only: each contact's name and birthday. It does not read phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, notes or pictures. The list is read on your device and shown to you; only the birthdays you tick are saved as events, in the app's storage on this device. Nothing from your Contacts is uploaded, sent to us or shared with anyone, and the app never changes your Contacts. If you say no, everything else works as before, and you can change the permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Photos

With T-Minus Unlimited you can put your own photo behind a countdown. You choose the picture in the system photo picker, so the app receives only that one picture and never sees the rest of your library. The picture you pick is cropped and copied into the app's own storage on this device, with a smaller copy in the storage area shared with the T-Minus: Days Until Countdown widget so the widget can show it. These copies are not uploaded, not analysed on a server and not sent to us or anyone else; a picture leaves the device only if you share a countdown image yourself. Removing the photo or deleting the event deletes both copies; your original in the Photos library is never changed. Backup files exported from the app do not contain photos.

Purchases

T-Minus: Days Until Countdown sells two optional one-time purchases through Apple's App Store: T-Minus Unlimited and the Christmas & New Year background pack. A monthly subscription from the first version of the app is no longer offered in the app; people who already have it keep their access while it is active and can manage or cancel it in their Apple account settings. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details or your Apple account. The app asks the App Store on your device whether you own a purchase in order to unlock what you paid for; nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

If you add a T-Minus: Days Until Countdown widget, the app shares your event names, dates and chosen emoji with the widget through a storage area on this device that only T-Minus: Days Until Countdown and its widget can read. Nothing leaves the device.

Sharing and export

If you use the share or export feature, the content you choose leaves the app only to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). We do not receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Device backups (iCloud, Google) may include app data according to your own backup settings.

Children

T-Minus: Days Until Countdown is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data in a new way (for example adding crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
