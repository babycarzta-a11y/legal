Privacy policy — Mileage Log (Fahrtenbuch · Rittenregistratie · Kilometrówka)

Effective 7 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing reaches us. Vehicles, odometer readings, trips, addresses, purposes, business partners, drivers and the holder details for the printed sheet are stored in one file on your iPhone. There is no account, no server that receives your entries, and no analytics or advertising SDK. The only network traffic is the App Store purchase handled by Apple and links you open yourself.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, photos, location, contacts or notifications.

The PDF

The month sheet is created on the phone. It goes only where you send it with the share sheet.

Purchases

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID.

Storage and deletion

Deleting the app deletes the data. An iCloud device backup may contain it according to your own backup settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
