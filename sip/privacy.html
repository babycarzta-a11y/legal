Sip Daily: privacy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 (version 1.1)

Short version: your drink log never leaves your iPhone unless you send it somewhere yourself. The developer receives nothing.

Your log

Drinks, amounts, times, your goal, your drink shares, saved cups and reminder settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics and no advertising code in Sip Daily.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications planned by iOS on your device. Their text never contains your amounts.

Widgets

To show today's amount on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, the app shares a small summary (today's counted amount, goal, unit and the widget's labels) with its own widget through an App Group container on the same device. The widget's "+" button stores the drinks you add there until the app moves them into your log.

Apple Health

Only if you switch it on, Sip Daily writes the counted amount of each drink to Water in Apple Health, and updates or removes that sample when you edit or delete the entry. Sip Daily requests write access only and never reads health data. Data in Apple Health is governed by Apple; you can revoke access in the Health app at any time. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Export

The CSV export (Sip+) creates a file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.

Purchase

Sip+ is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The app only asks the App Store on your device whether Sip+ is owned; payment details are never visible to the app or the developer.

Deleting your data

Delete an entry in the app, or delete the app to remove the whole log. Samples written to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. iCloud device backups may contain app data according to your own backup settings.

Children

Sip Daily is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
