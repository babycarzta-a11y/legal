Aura support

Aura is a headache diary that counts days the way headache clinics do: headache days, migraine days and days with an acute medicine. It keeps everything on your iPhone, with no account and no server.

Question, problem or idea? Email babycar.zta@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have. You will get a reply from a person, usually within two working days.

During an attack

On Today, tap Attack started. The timer runs until you tap It's over. An attack can stay open for days (up to 90), so long attacks are counted on every day they touch. Fill in the rest later under Details: migraine or other headache, aura, worst pain from 1 to 5, possible triggers and a note.

I took a medicine logs a dose. Two hours later Aura asks once on Today how it worked: no better, better, or pain-free. Aura sends no notifications, so the question waits until you next open the app, and it stops asking after a week.

Medication days

Under My medicines, give each medicine a name and a group. Aura counts days, not tablets: two doses on one day are one day. The lines come from the International Classification of Headache Disorders (ICHD-3):

GroupLine
Simple painkillers (paracetamol, aspirin, ibuprofen and other NSAIDs)15 days a month
Triptans, ergots, opioids, combination painkillers10 days a month
Any mix of the groups above10 days a month
Gepants, ditans, other acute, preventivescounted, no line

Aura shows the last 30 days and whether a line was reached in each of the three previous months. It is a count to take to your clinician, not a diagnosis. Do not stop or change a medicine because of it without talking to them.

Aura Pro tools

Treatment check

Add the preventive and the day you started it. Aura compares migraine days per 28 days in the 28, 56 or 84 days before the start with the latest 28 days on it, and shows the change as a band: 50% or more fewer, 30–49% fewer, less than 30%, or more. It needs at least 14 days of diary before the start and 28 days on treatment.

Relief at 2 hours

For each acute medicine: how many doses you answered, the share with relief and the share pain-free at 2 hours. Fewer than three answers are shown but not compared.

Cycle window

Turn it on in Preferences and log the first day of each period. Aura checks for migraine days from 2 days before to 3 days after each start and says in how many of the last three cycles there was one. It also predicts the next window from your usual cycle length.

Clinic sheet

A PDF with one page per month (1, 3 or 6 months): a day grid marked M, H, A and acute medicine, the totals and the medication-day lines, then a page with your medicines, treatments and relief at 2 hours. You can also export a CSV with one row per day.

Free and Pro

Free, with no time limit: the attack timer, the day ledger, medication days and your full history. Aura Pro is an annual subscription that adds the four tools above. Restore a purchase under Preferences › Subscription. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Your data

Everything is stored on the iPhone only. Aura has no account, no analytics and no network calls of its own. Files you create (PDF, CSV) leave the phone only when you share them. Preferences › Erase the diary removes everything. Deleting the app also deletes the data, so share a CSV first if you want a copy.

Updating from Aura 1.0: your earlier entries are kept and shown as finished attacks; their triggers and pain levels carry over.

Common questions

Why is a day red when I only had a short headache?

Any attack that touches a day makes it a headache day. That is how clinics count.

Can I fill in last month?

Yes. Open the Day ledger, go to the month and tap a day to add an attack or a medicine on it.

Does Aura tell me what to take?

No. Aura records and counts; it never recommends a medicine or a dose.
