Privacy Policy — Wheel: Decision Spinner

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Wheel: Decision Spinner is built so that your data stays with you. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you enter in Wheel: Decision Spinner is stored locally on your device. We do not run user accounts, we do not have a server that receives your entries, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Device backups (iCloud, Google) may include app data according to your own backup settings.

Children

Wheel: Decision Spinner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data in a new way (for example adding crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
