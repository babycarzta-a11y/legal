Privacy Policy — Tacit

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Tacit is built so that your data stays with you. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you enter in Tacit is stored locally on your device. We do not run user accounts, we do not have a server that receives your entries, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Optional paid features are sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play — depending on the app, as a one-time purchase or a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details or your Apple/Google account. The app asks the store on your device whether you own the purchase in order to unlock the features you paid for; nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

App lock (Face ID / passcode)

If you turn on the optional app lock, Tacit asks iOS to verify you with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. The check is performed entirely by iOS; the app only receives a yes/no answer and never has access to your biometric data or passcode. App content is also hidden in the app switcher.

Your two phones

Tacit connects two phones that belong to a couple. To link them, both phones show the same 6-digit code and you confirm it on each phone. Before that, only a public key is exchanged — no names and no entries. After linking, everything that goes from one phone to the other is end-to-end encrypted (X25519, XChaCha20-Poly1305) with a key that exists only on your two phones.

Bluetooth

When both apps are open and the phones are near each other, they exchange encrypted cards over Bluetooth directly, without the internet and without any server. Bluetooth is not used for tracking or location.

Envelopes

If you send an envelope, the app creates an encrypted text that you forward yourself through any messenger. The messenger only sees unreadable ciphertext; only your partner's linked phone can open it. We never receive it.

What your partner sees

Your partner sees only what you explicitly choose to share. Personal entries, your check-ins and your cycle diary stay on your phone unless you pick a sharing level yourself. A hint never reveals its card unless your partner asks; a green light is shown only when both lights are on.

Cycle diary — not a medical device

Tacit can keep a simple diary of your period dates. Estimates are made only from your own entries on your device. The app does not diagnose, predict fertility or replace advice from a doctor, and cycle data is never sent to us or used for advertising.

Encryption on your phone

The app database is encrypted (SQLCipher) with a key kept in the iOS Keychain on this device, and the database file is excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

Age

Tacit is intended for adults (17+).

Sharing and export

If you use the share or export feature, the content you choose leaves the app only to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). We do not receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. The encrypted database is excluded from device backups, so it is not restored to a new phone. Unlinking the phones deletes the pair key; cards you have already received stay on your phone until you delete them or the app.

Children

Tacit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data in a new way (for example adding crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
