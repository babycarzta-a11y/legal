Privacy Policy — Rounds: HIIT Timer

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Rounds: HIIT Timer is built so that your data stays with you. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you enter in Rounds: HIIT Timer is stored locally on your device. We do not run user accounts, we do not have a server that receives your entries, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Workout log

Since version 1.1 Rounds keeps a log of the workouts you finish or stop (program name, start and end time, active and work time, intervals). It is stored only on your iPhone. You can delete any entry by swiping it away, and CSV export happens only when you tap Export and choose where the file goes.

Apple Health

If you turn on Settings > Apple Health > Save workouts to Health, iOS asks for permission and Rounds then writes each logged session to Apple Health as a workout (type, start, end, pauses). Rounds never reads anything from Apple Health, and health data is not used for advertising or sent anywhere. Turning the switch off stops new writes; workouts already saved stay in Apple Health, where you can delete them.

Voice cues and the Lock Screen

Spoken cues use the speech voices built into iOS on your device. The Lock Screen countdown is a Live Activity drawn by iOS from data the app hands it locally; nothing is sent to a server and no push service is used.

Purchases

Optional paid features are sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play — depending on the app, as a one-time purchase or a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details or your Apple/Google account. The app asks the store on your device whether you own the purchase in order to unlock the features you paid for; nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Device backups (iCloud, Google) may include app data according to your own backup settings.

Children

Rounds: HIIT Timer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data in a new way (for example adding crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
