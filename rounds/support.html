R1 Programs and the builder

How is a program put together?

A program is an optional get ready lead-in followed by one or more blocks. A block is a list of steps (each one work or rest, with its own name and length) that repeats for the number of rounds you set. Two kinds of rest sit around a block:

  • Rest between rounds goes between two repeats of the same block. If the repeat already ends with a rest step, the longer block rest takes its place, so you never get rest followed by rest.
  • Rest after block goes before the next block. Nothing is added after the last block.

A workout never ends on a rest: trailing rests are dropped. The coloured strip on every card is the program's real timeline, tall bars for work and short ones for rest.

Can I change a ready-made program?

Open it and tap Copy and edit. The copy becomes one of your own programs; the original stays as it is. Ready-made programs follow the app's language, so a copy keeps the names in the language you made it in.

What happened to my 1.0 presets?

On the first start of 1.1 each saved preset becomes one of your own programs with the same work, rest, rounds and get-ready times and the same name. Nothing is deleted.

Limits

Up to 12 blocks, 20 steps per block, 99 rounds per block, 60 minutes per step, 5 minutes of get ready and 6 hours per program. Back and Skip on the run screen move by whole steps; skipped work is not counted as done in your log.

R3 Sound, voice and music

Will Rounds stop my music or podcast?

No. Beeps and the voice play over whatever is playing, at full music volume. If you prefer the music to dip for each cue, turn on Settings > Sound > Lower music during cues. Cues also sound with the Ring/Silent switch set to silent.

What does the voice coach say, and in which language?

It says the step name when a step starts ("Squats", or "Go" for a plain work step), "Rest. Next: push-ups" when a rest starts, counts 3-2-1, and, if the program asks for it, "Halfway" and "Ten seconds". It speaks the app's language using the voices built into iOS. If a voice sounds robotic, download a better one in iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices. You can switch the voice off for all programs in Settings, or per program in the builder (useful for routines full of short holds).

Why are there no sounds after I lock the phone?

While a workout runs, Rounds keeps the screen on so you can see the clock and hear every cue. If you lock the phone or switch apps, iOS pauses the app's sounds; the timer keeps counting by the clock and is exactly in the right place when you come back. The Lock Screen countdown (below) keeps showing time while the phone is locked.

R4 Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

What does the Live Activity show?

The current step, its countdown, the round, what comes next and the time to finish. iOS draws both countdowns itself. When a step ends while the phone is locked, iOS cannot know the next step, so the card shows an "open the app" arrow instead of a wrong time; the time to finish stays correct. Needs iOS 16.2 or later; switch it in Settings > Lock Screen or in iOS Settings > Rounds > Live Activities.

R5 Log, week streak and Apple Health

How does the week streak count?

You set a weekly goal (1 to 7 sessions). Each week that reaches it extends the streak; the current week counts as soon as you reach the goal and does not break the streak while it is still running. Weeks start on the first day of the week of your region. Sessions under 30 seconds are not logged.

What goes to Apple Health?

Only if you turn on Settings > Apple Health: each logged session is saved as a workout with its type (HIIT, boxing, strength, cardio, core or mobility, chosen per program), start, end and pauses. Rounds reads nothing from Health. To stop, turn the switch off or remove access in the Health app: Profile > Apps > Rounds.

Can I delete or export the log?

Swipe a session to the left to delete it. With Premium, Export CSV gives a spreadsheet file (date, start, end, program, type, active and work seconds, intervals done and planned, completed) through the share sheet. The log never leaves your phone otherwise.

R6 Rounds Premium

What is free and what is Premium?

Free: the Quick timer, all 14 ready-made programs, the builder with up to 3 own programs, voice cues, the Lock Screen countdown, the log with week streaks, Apple Health, light and dark mode, 20 languages. Premium adds unlimited own programs, the Bell, Buzzer and Gong sound packs, and CSV export. There are no ads and no account.

Price, renewal and cancelling

Rounds Premium is an annual auto-renewing subscription; the App Store shows the price in your currency before you confirm. Cancel any time, at least 24 hours before renewal, in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or from Settings > Manage subscription in the app. If Premium lapses, your oldest 3 programs stay usable and the others are kept, locked, until you renew or delete them.

I paid but Premium is not active

Tap Restore purchase in Settings or on the Premium page while signed in to the same Apple Account. If that does not help, email me the date of purchase (no card details needed).
