What this app is

Tape Calculator is a desktop adding machine for the iPhone: every entry is printed on a paper tape that grows out of a slot, and every line can be tapped and changed afterwards, with everything below recalculated at once. Numbers are exact (no floating-point rounding), so 10 ÷ 3 × 3 prints 10 and 0.1 + 0.2 prints 0.3. It works offline, without an account or ads. A tape looks like this:

Rent1 250.00
+ Electricity86.40
− Deposit returned−200.00
+ VAT 19 %215.92
Total1 352.32

The keys, briefly

+ − × ÷ work as on any calculator in the default Calculator typing style (operator first: 120 + 30 − 5 =). ◇ prints a subtotal and carries on. = (✱) prints the total and starts a new calculation. GT adds up all totals since the last GT. %: 200 + 10 % gives 220, 200 − 15 % gives 170, 200 × 10 % gives 20. +VAT adds tax to the amount so far, −VAT takes it out of a gross amount and shows the net; hold either key to change the rate. Markup and Margin: enter the cost, press +, type the percentage, then press the key (markup is on cost, margin is the share of the selling price). M+ M− MR MC are the memory; an M on the display means it is not empty. CE clears the number you are typing; hold it to clear the whole tape (undo is offered). The same text is in the app under How it works.

Questions

I made a typo three lines up. Do I have to start over? No. Tap the line. You can change its number, operator or comment, insert a line above or below it, or delete it. Every result underneath is recomputed immediately, because the tape stores only what you typed and recalculates the rest from the top.
Why does 2 + 3 × 4 give 14 and not 20? By default × and ÷ bind first, so line items like 3 × 4.50 + 2 × 1.20 add up the way they are written on an invoice. If you prefer a simple pocket calculator that goes left to right (2 + 3 × 4 = 20), switch Order of operations to Left to right in Settings.
What is the "Adding machine" typing style? On a desktop adding machine you type the number first and then + or −: 120 + 30 + 5 − ✱. Settings → Typing style → Adding machine turns that on. × and ÷ still work as on a calculator, and = prints the total.
The key says "Tax" (or "GST", "MwSt.", "TVA") instead of VAT. The key uses the word printed on receipts in your language and region: Tax in the US, GST in Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, India and Canada, VAT elsewhere in English, MwSt., TVA, IVA and so on in other languages. The default rate also follows your region (for example 19 % in Germany, 20 % in France, 25.5 % in Finland). The US and Brazil start with no rate and ask for one the first time you press the key.
I changed the VAT rate but old lines kept the old one. That is on purpose. Every VAT and percent line stores the rate it was printed with, so changing the default later does not silently rewrite an older tape. Tap the line to change its rate.
Why do the printed amounts not add up to the total exactly? Check Decimal places in Settings. In F (floating) mode results are exact and shown with up to 10 decimals, so a rounded display can look off by a cent. In fixed mode (0–4 places) every computed amount is rounded when it is finished, so the printed lines always add up to the printed totals. The rounding rule is yours: 5/4 half up (the usual rule for money), banker's rounding, always up, or always down.
Can I use a comma as the decimal mark? Yes. The tape follows your region's number format (1.234,56; 1 234,56; 1'234.50; 12,34,567) or a fixed choice in Settings, and text fields accept both marks.
How do I name a tape, add a heading or a comment? Use Rename tape from the menu, Add a heading line for a line like "March" or "Kitchen", and tap any line to give it a comment such as "Rent" or "Taxi". Tapes have dates, and the Tapes screen searches names, comments and numbers.
Is there a printer sound? Yes, a soft print sound for each line, off by default (Settings → Printer sound). It is played as a system sound, so the silent switch mutes it and it never interrupts music. Key clicks (haptics) are on by default.
Does it sync with iCloud or my iPad? No. There is no server and the app is iPhone only. Your tapes live in a file on this iPhone, which is included in your device backup. Back up to a file in Settings writes all tapes to one file you can keep in Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop to another iPhone; Import a backup adds tapes from such a file without deleting anything.

Pro is a single non-consumable in-app purchase, product id tapecalc_pro. It is not a subscription and never renews. The price on the paywall is whatever the App Store reports for your storefront; the app does not carry a price of its own. Nothing that is free today will become paid.

Restore. After reinstalling or moving to a new iPhone, open Settings → Restore purchases (the paywall has a Restore link too). The app asks the App Store for your purchases under the Apple ID signed in on the phone and switches Pro back on. If it answers "No earlier purchase was found for this Apple ID", you are signed in with a different account than the one that bought it. The unlock is also re-checked quietly on every cold start; a store that cannot be reached never switches Pro off.

Losing Pro (for instance after a family member's Apple ID signs in) never deletes or hides a tape. You keep everything; the app only stops you adding a second tape until Pro is restored.

Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves because we never see it.

If something is wrong

"The App Store isn't available right now" The phone has no connection to the App Store, is signed out of Media & Purchases, or in-app purchases are blocked by Screen Time. Fix one of those and open the paywall again.
"Division by zero" or "The margin must be under 100 %" on the tape The error line stays on the tape so you can see where it happened; it is confined to that calculation and does not break the rest. Tap the line and fix the number.
Import says "This is not a Tape Calculator backup" Only the file created by Back up to a file can be imported, not a PDF, CSV or text export.
"1 tape was left out: the free version keeps one tape" The backup had more tapes than the free version can hold. Import it again after unlocking Pro and the rest comes in.
A line was deleted by mistake Tap Undo on the message right after. The same applies to a cleared tape and a deleted tape.
