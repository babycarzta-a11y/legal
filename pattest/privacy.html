Privacy Policy — PAT Register

Last updated: 3 October 2026

PAT Register is built so that your work stays yours. This page says plainly what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing reaches us. Everything you enter — clients, site addresses, appliances, readings, results, notes, your own name and company — stays on your device in a single file. There are no user accounts, there is no server that receives your entries, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDK. The app makes no network requests at all apart from the purchase itself, which Apple handles.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, no photo library, no location, no contacts, no notifications.

The documents it prints

The certificate, the register and the label sheet are produced entirely on your phone. We never see them and have no access to them. If you use the share button, the PDF goes only where you send it — Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or a printer.

Purchases

Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, that unlocks the PDF register and certificate, the label sheet and more than one client. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or your Apple Account. The app asks the store on your device whether the purchase belongs to you, so it can unlock the feature. Nothing about the purchase reaches us.

Storage and deletion

Your register lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. A device backup (iCloud) may include it, according to your own backup settings.

Children

PAT Register is a tool for working electricians. It is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no information from them.

Disclaimer

PAT Register records and prints what you enter. It is not a test instrument, it does not certify equipment by itself, and it does not replace your judgement or your competence as the person carrying out the inspection and testing. The limits it shows are the usual values from the IET Code of Practice, offered as a prompt; the result of every test is set by you.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data in a new way, this page will be updated before that happens, and the date above will change.
