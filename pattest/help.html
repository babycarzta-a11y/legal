Help — PAT Register

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Getting started

Add the client first — the name and site address are printed on the register and on the certificate. Then add the appliances you are testing there: the ID you write on the label, what it is, where it lives, its class and how often it is retested. Open an appliance and tap Record a test to enter the readings and set PASS or FAIL.

Why can't I edit a test?

Because a register that can be quietly adjusted is worth nothing to the client. Tap Correct on a record: the app writes a new record that replaces the old one, the register shows the corrected values, and the original stays in the appliance's history marked as corrected.

Where does the retest date come from?

From the interval set on the appliance, counted from the last test. The interval belongs to your risk assessment, not to a fixed rule, so each appliance can have its own. Overdue appliances and those due within a month are counted at the top of the register.

The app says a reading is outside the limits — can I still pass it?

Yes. The warning compares your reading with the usual limits for that class in the IET Code of Practice. It is a prompt, not a verdict: you carried out the test and your name goes on the certificate, so the result is yours to set.

Class II appliances

A Class II appliance is double insulated and has no protective earth, so the app does not ask for an earth continuity reading and applies the higher insulation limit.

Printing

Tap Register, certificate and labels. The register prints as one table covering every appliance for that client, with a signed certificate page in front of it; failures are red and are listed again in words. Labels print three to a row on A4 — appliance ID, result, test date, retest date and tester. A failed appliance gets DO NOT USE on its label.

What is free and what is Pro

Recording tests and reading the register on screen are free, for one client and any number of appliances. Pro — £4.99 a month or £34.99 a year — adds the PDF register and certificate, the label sheet and further clients. There is no second paywall for getting your own data out.

Restoring a purchase

New phone or reinstalled app: open Register, certificate and labels or the tester details screen and tap Restore purchase.

Backups

The register is stored on the device and is included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have one. It is not stored anywhere else, so keep a backup on if the records matter to you — and print the register for each client as you go.
