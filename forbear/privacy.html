Privacy Policy — Dreamward

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Dreamward is built so that your data stays with you. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you enter in Dreamward is stored locally on your device. We do not run user accounts, we do not have a server that receives your entries, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Notifications

Dreamward can send local reminders that you set up. These are scheduled on your device; no notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

Optional paid features are sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play — depending on the app, as a one-time purchase or a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details or your Apple/Google account. The app asks the store on your device whether you own the purchase in order to unlock the features you paid for; nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

Photos

Dreamward asks for access to your photo library only when you choose a picture yourself. The picture you pick is copied into the app's own storage on this device. It is not uploaded, not analysed on a server, and not shared with anyone unless you use a share feature yourself. The app never reads your library in the background and never sees photos you did not pick.

Sound and vibration

Dreamward can play a short tone and vibrate when you record something. Both are produced on the device, both can be turned off in the app's settings, and the phone's silent switch mutes the sound. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

Sharing and export

If you use the share or export feature, the content you choose leaves the app only to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). We do not receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Device backups (iCloud, Google) may include app data according to your own backup settings.

Children

Dreamward is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data in a new way (for example adding crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
