Annum

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy covers the Annum camera app for iPhone. In short: the app collects no personal data, has no account and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Every picture is developed on your iPhone. The same policy is published at annumcamera.pages.dev/privacy.

Camera

Annum asks for camera access so you can take photographs. Each picture is developed on your device, in the year you chose. The silent-film camera records video frames without sound; the microphone is never used. Photos and videos are never uploaded or analysed on a server. If you decline camera access, you can still develop a photo you pick from your library.

Photos library

Annum saves every finished photo and film to your Photos library, in an album called Annum. It asks for permission to add photos; if iOS asks for wider access in order to create the Annum album, Annum still never browses or reads your other photos. To develop an existing photo you pick it in the system photo picker, and the app receives only that picture; the original is never changed. The app keeps a copy of your last 50 pictures in its own storage on the device (the Roll).

Motion sensor

In the daguerreotype tilt viewer the app reads the phone’s tilt to show the plate as a positive or a negative. The reading is used only on screen and is never stored.

Notifications

The only notification Annum can send is an optional reminder before a free trial ends, if you start one and allow it. It is scheduled on your device; no notification server of ours is involved.

Purchases

Annum Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether you own Pro and keeps that status, and the free-shot counters, in the device Keychain. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us.

Sharing

When you use the share button, the picture goes only to the destination you choose. When you share a film, suggested hashtags are placed on your clipboard; nothing is read from it.

Retention and deletion

App data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes the Roll and settings; Keychain items such as the purchase status and free-shot counters can remain on the device by design. Your Photos library keeps the pictures you saved. Device backups follow your own iCloud settings.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to answer you. Ask us and we delete the conversation.

Children

Annum is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data in a new way, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
