Help — Fire Risk Assessment

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Getting started

Add the premises first: the name, the address and the responsible person — the person who by law must keep the building safe from fire. Then tap Start assessment and work through the sections as you walk the building.

Answering a question

Each question opens with a short note on what to look at and what is usually accepted. Answer Yes, No or N/A and write what you saw. An answer of No offers to add an action to the plan, with the wording already written.

Premises where people sleep

Switch on People sleep here on the premises and extra questions appear: detection in sleeping rooms and the spaces they open onto, night-time arrangements, and the yearly fire safety information a responsible person must give residents.

The risk rating

Likelihood of a fire against the consequence for people gives one of five ratings, from Trivial to Intolerable, each with plain words for what it means. A target date is suggested from the rating, and you can change it. The app never sets the rating for you.

The action plan

Actions are listed highest risk first, with a target date and a name against each. Mark one done with a note of what was done; overdue actions are counted at the top of the main screen. The plan stays live after the report has been signed off.

Signing off

Signing off locks the answers, so the report you handed to the client keeps matching what is in the app. You can reopen it if something has to change, and actions can be marked done either way. A new visit is a new assessment — the old one stays.

The report

The PDF prints the conclusion, the action plan and every question with your answer and notes. If the assessment is not finished — questions unanswered, no risk rating, an action without a date — the first page says so, rather than pretending otherwise.

What is free and what is Pro

One set of premises, the whole walk-round and the action plan are free, with no limits. Pro — £4.99 a month or £34.99 a year — adds the PDF report and further premises. There is no second paywall.

Restoring a purchase

New phone or reinstalled app: open Report for the client or the assessor details screen and tap Restore purchase.

What this app is not

It is not a fire risk assessor and it certifies nothing. It records your findings and prints them. Where premises are complex, or you are not confident, use a competent assessor.
