Privacy Policy — Fire Risk Assessment

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Fire Risk Assessment is built so that your work stays yours. This page says plainly what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing reaches us. Everything you enter — premises, the responsible person, your answers, notes, findings, actions and dates, your own name and company — stays on your device in a single file. There are no user accounts, there is no server that receives your entries, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDK. The app makes no network requests at all apart from the purchase itself, which Apple handles.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, no photo library, no location, no contacts, no notifications.

The report

The PDF report is produced entirely on your phone. We never see it and have no access to it. If you use the share button, the file goes only where you send it — Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop or a printer.

Purchases

Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, that unlocks the PDF report and more than one set of premises. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or your Apple Account. The app asks the store on your device whether the purchase belongs to you, so it can unlock the feature. Nothing about the purchase reaches us.

Storage and deletion

Your assessments live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of them. A device backup (iCloud) may include them, according to your own backup settings.

Children

The app is a tool for people responsible for premises. It is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no information from them.

Disclaimer

Fire Risk Assessment records and prints what you enter. It is not a fire risk assessor, it certifies nothing, and it does not make premises compliant with the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. Every answer, every risk rating and every target date is set by the user, who remains responsible for the assessment. Where premises are complex, or you are not confident, use a competent assessor.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data in a new way, this page will be updated before that happens, and the date above will change.
