Support

Having trouble with one of our apps, found a bug, or have an idea? Email babycar.zta@gmail.com and include the app name and your device model. We reply within a few business days.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. To cancel or get a refund: iPhone — Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android — Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions.

Your data

All our apps keep your data on your device only — see each app's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes its data.
