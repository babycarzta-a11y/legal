How to play

  1. Add the players. Type each name on the setup screen and tap Add. Two people are enough; the free game seats up to six.
  2. Choose packs and a mode. Tick one or more packs, then pick Player's choice, Truths only, Dares only or Random.
  3. Tap START, then SPIN. The wheel (or the bottle, with the Bottle theme) stops on the next player.
  4. Truth or dare? In Player's choice the player decides; the card flips over. Dares that have a timer show a Start button with the seconds; you can pause and resume it.
  5. DONE or PASS. Passing is always allowed and is simply counted, so nobody is pushed into anything.

Fair turns

With Fair turns on, nobody is picked twice before everyone has had a turn. Players who already went are dimmed on the wheel; their slices keep the same size.

Will I see the same card again?

Not until you have been through every card of that kind in the packs you chose; then the deck reshuffles and tells you so. To start fresh any time: Backstage → Reset card history.

Favourites and your own cards

Tap the heart on a card during a game to keep it under Favourites. Under My cards you can write your own truths and dares, with an optional timer; they play as their own pack. The free game holds 10 of your own cards.

The packs

There are 960 cards in 8 packs, 120 in each, half truths and half dares. Every card was written by hand for this game and is translated into all 20 languages the app speaks.

Touch dares

Some dares involve gentle contact, such as a hug or a high five. Each of those cards is marked in the deck.

Allow touch dares is a switch on the setup screen, below the packs and the mode. Turn it off and every marked dare is removed from the deck before the game starts, in every pack, including Couples. It is on by default; turning it off is a good idea with kids, classmates or people who have just met.

The Backstage Pass

The Backstage Pass (Pro) is the game's only purchase. You pay once; it is not a subscription and never renews. There are no ads anywhere in the game, with or without the pass.

What it opens

  • All 8 packs (the 5 marked PASS above)
  • Unlimited players and unlimited cards of your own
  • The Stage and Bottle themes
  • The Twist slice on the wheel: group truths, group dares, compliment rounds and seat swaps
  • Scoring (truth +1, dare +2) with a leaderboard
  • Saved player lists for the people you play with often

Where do I get it?

Tap any locked pack, the Twist or Scoring switch, or Backstage → Your pass. The price is shown on the button, in your currency, as the App Store sets it. Payment is handled by Apple.

Restoring the pass on a new or reset iPhone

Open Backstage (the ticket at the top right of the setup screen) and tap Restore purchase on your pass, or tap Restore purchase under the button on the pass screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. Restoring is free.

If it says nothing was found, check that you are signed in to the App Store with that Apple ID (Settings → your name → Media & Purchases), then try again. For refunds, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot issue them ourselves.

Other questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Every card is inside the app, so it plays in a tent, on a plane or at a cabin with no signal. Only buying or restoring the pass talks to the App Store.

What does it keep, and where?

Player names, saved lists, your own cards, favourites and settings are stored only on this iPhone. There is no sign-up and nothing is sent to us. Details: privacy policy.

Can I turn off the sound or the buzz?

Yes: Backstage → At the table has Sound and Haptics. Sounds mix with your music instead of stopping it.

The app is in the wrong language

It follows your iPhone language. To change only this game: iPhone Settings → Apps → Truth or Dare → Language.
